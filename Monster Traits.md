Each monster page lists the monster's traits under **Classifications**, such as flying, carnivore or thick hide.

## 🛡️ Thick Hide

A monster with a thick hide takes none of a weapon's own damage if the weapon is made of leather or a softer material, such as cloth. Its hide does not reduce the damage of weapons made of wood, bone or metal, arrows and bolts included. Most dragons and nagas, and many dinosaurs and golems, have a thick hide.
