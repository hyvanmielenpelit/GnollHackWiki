## Combat Skill

Gives bonus to-hit and to-damage when no weapon is wielded. Can be used with weapon gauntlets.

You do not have to reach Grand Master level here before enhancing [[/Skills/martial arts]]; that only needs enough training and free skill slots. What Grand Master level changes is the training: unarmed hits train bare-handed combat until it reaches Grand Master and train martial arts only after that, while kicks train martial arts at any time.


| Skill Level | Skill Slots to Advance | Training to Advance | To-Hit Bonus | Damage Bonus | Critical Strike Chance |
| :---------- | :--------------------: | :-----------------: | :----------: | :----------: | :--------------------: |
| **Unskilled** | | | 0 | 0 | 0% |
| **Basic** | 1 | 20 | +3 | +2 | 5% |
| **Skilled** | 1 | 80 | +6 | +4 | 10% |
| **Expert** | 1 | 180 | +9 | +6 | 20% |
| **Master** | 2 | 320 | +12 | +8 | 35% |
| **Grand Master** | 2 | 500 | +15 | +10 | 50% |
