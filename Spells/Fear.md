## Level 3 enchantment spell

- **Attributes:** Higher of intelligence and charisma
- **Mana cost:** 20.0
- **Casting time:** 1 round
- **Cooldown:** None
- **Targeting:** Multiple targets in selected direction
- **Range:** 50'
- **Duration:** 1d25+50 rounds
- **Saving throw:** Against wisdom — see [[/Saving Throws]]
- **Extra skill penalty:** -10% per skill level above Unskilled — see [[/Saving Throws]]
- **Save adjustment:** +0
- **Train chance:** 100%
- **Base write cost:** 50 charges
- **Write cost:** From half to full base cost
- **Components:** Verbal, Material

## Material components - 15 castings

1. a mandrake root

## Description

Causes monsters to flee. Monsters that are immune to fear, along with all undead and all mindless monsters, are unaffected before any saving throw is rolled; the same is true of a monster that has gained fear resistance, has no mind to frighten, or is a vampire in shifted form. Every other monster makes the wisdom saving throw described above.
