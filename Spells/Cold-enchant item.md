## Level 5 enchantment spell

- **Attributes:** Higher of intelligence and charisma
- **Mana cost:** 80.0
- **Casting time:** 1 round
- **Cooldown:** None
- **Targeting:** None
- **Train chance:** 100%
- **Base write cost:** 70 charges
- **Write cost:** From half the base cost to one charge less than the base cost
- **Components:** Verbal, Material

## Material components - 3 castings

1. a nugget of iron ore

## Description

Cold-enchants one suitable item: a weapon or a weapon-tool such as a pick-axe, but not a launcher such as a bow; any kind of gauntlets, shoes, kicking boots, spiked boots or a spiked shield, which also fight as weapons; a rock, flint stone or pebble; or a bag or quiver that makes ammunition. From a stack, it enchants at most three. On a fire-enchanted item, it removes the fire enchantment instead. See [[/Elemental Enchantments]].
