## Level 6 celestial spell

- **Attributes:** Higher of wisdom and charisma
- **Mana cost:** 60.0
- **Casting time:** 1 round
- **Cooldown:** None
- **Targeting:** None
- **Train chance:** 100%
- **Base write cost:** 80 charges
- **Write cost:** From half the base cost to one charge less than the base cost
- **Components:** Verbal, Material

## Material components - 1 casting

1. a noncursed ruby

## Description

Summons a permanent [[/Monsters/ki-rin]], which comes with a saddle. A ki-rin has 16 hit dice and gains levels by killing only up to level 24; see [[/Monsters that Grow Up]].
