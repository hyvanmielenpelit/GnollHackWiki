## Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you can ask for access to your data, its correction or deletion, a restriction of its use, or a copy of it, and you can object to its processing or withdraw your consent. You can also lodge a complaint with the [Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman](https://tietosuoja.fi/en) in Finland. The full policies above describe these rights and how to use them for each service.
