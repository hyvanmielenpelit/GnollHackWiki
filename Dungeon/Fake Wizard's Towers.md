> 👉 **The Fake Wizard's Towers are two nearly identical decoy structures found deep in Gehennom. One holds the magic portal leading to the true [[/Dungeon/Wizard's Tower]], while the other holds a randomly generated amulet.**

**Location:** Both towers are generated between levels 13 and 18 of [[/Dungeon/Gehennom]] (specifically, between 2 and 5 levels above the bottom of Gehennom). 

## ℹ️ Characteristics

Both fake towers appear from the outside as small structures surrounded by a moat. There is no way to tell which of the two towers is which from the outside. 

One of the two towers contains a magic portal that transports you directly to the bottom level of the true [[/Dungeon/Wizard's Tower]]. The other tower contains a randomly generated amulet. 

Each tower is walled all around with no door, so crossing the moat is not enough: you have to get through the wall, for example by digging. Arriving by stairs, by falling or by level teleport never puts you inside a tower. In the tower with the portal, the portal is hidden on the center square, where a member of the lich family waits beside a vampire lord, with squeaky boards on the squares around it and a kraken in the moat.
