This policy describes the personal data we collect when you register and use a GnollHack account and the account website, [account.gnollhack.com](https://account.gnollhack.com), why, to whom it goes, and for how long, and the rights you have over it. The data that the GnollHack app sends to your account is described in the [[GnollHack App Privacy Policy]], and the data of the Gnoll Overseer in the [[Gnoll Overseer Privacy Policy]]. Our other privacy policies are listed on the [[Privacy Policy]] page.

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1. Registration and Sign-In

When you register an account with us, we store the following information as your unique identifiers:

- **User name**
- **Email address**

This information is used for authentication and account management. We also store:

- Your password, only as a hash, never in readable form
- If you turn on two-factor sign-in, the key of your authenticator app and your recovery codes
- The time your account was created and the time you last signed in, from the website or from the game
- The version of the Terms of Service you accepted
- Your Junethack user name, if you add one to your account

The cookies the website uses for signing in are listed in the [[GnollHack Account Cookie Policy]].

### 2.2. Account Email

The website sends you email to confirm your email address and to reset your password. If a request to delete your account needs review, our administrators receive an email about it, and you receive an email when the deletion is completed or declined.

### 2.3. Request Logs

For each request from the GnollHack app, the server records your IP address, your user name, the address of the request, the data sent, and the result. It also records the IP address of failed sign-ins.

### 2.4. Game Data From the App

The scores, dumplogs, bones files, save file tracking records, replays, and saved games that the GnollHack app sends while you are signed in to your account are described in the [[GnollHack App Privacy Policy]].

### 2.5. Website Analytics

account.gnollhack.com uses Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to count visits and see how the website is used. These tags load only if you accept optional cookies in the cookie notice.

If you accept, Google receives:

- The addresses of the pages you view and of the page before, without account identifiers, codes or email addresses
- A random identifier kept in the Google Analytics cookies
- Information about your device and browser
- The IP address that every request carries, from which Google derives an approximate location

According to Google, Google Analytics does not store individual IP addresses: It uses the address to derive the approximate location and discards it after use.

You can change your choice at any time under **Cookie Settings** in the footer of the website. Details are in the [[GnollHack Account Cookie Policy]].

### 2.6. Error Reports

When the account website fails, it sends an error report to Sentry. A report contains the error and technical details of the request. Sign-in cookies and other credentials, and email addresses and codes in links, are removed before the report is sent, and the content of forms you submit is never included.

## 3. Purposes and Legal Bases

We use your data only for the purposes below. The legal basis of each is one of those in Article 6(1) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Contract**: The processing is needed to provide the account you have registered.
- **Legitimate interests**: The processing is needed for our legitimate interests, such as keeping the service working and secure, and those interests are not overridden by your rights. You can object to it (section 7).
- **Consent**: You have agreed to the processing, and you can withdraw your consent at any time.

| Purpose | Data | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|
| **Creating and running your account, sign-in, and account email** | Sections 2.1 and 2.2 | Contract |
| **Recording which Terms of Service version you accepted** | Section 2.1 | Legitimate interests: showing what you agreed to |
| **Security and fixing faults** | Request logs, failed sign-in records, and error reports (sections 2.3 and 2.6) | Legitimate interests: security and fixing faults |
| **Handling a deletion request that needs review** | The review email to our administrators (section 2.2) | Legitimate interests: handling your deletion request |
| **Website analytics** | Section 2.5 | Consent; withdraw it under **Cookie Settings** |

## 5. International Transfers

The GnollHack account server is in the United States, so your account data is transferred outside the European Economic Area. Account email is delivered by Microsoft Corporation, a company in the United States.

Error reports stay in the European Union: Sentry stores them in its European Union data region, in Germany.

Google may process analytics data on servers outside the European Union, including in the United States. Those transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses as the fallback.

## 6. Retention

| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| **Account data** (section 2.1) | Until you delete your account |
| **Request logs and failed sign-in records** | Indefinitely, as part of the server's security audit trail. When your account is deleted, your account and user name are removed from them. |

When your account is deleted, your user name, email address, and account details are deleted, and your game data is deleted or anonymized as described in the [[GnollHack App Privacy Policy]]. Your Gnoll Overseer data is deleted as described in the [[Gnoll Overseer Privacy Policy]].

## 7. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to:

- Get a copy of your personal data and information about how we use it (access)
- Have incorrect or incomplete data corrected (rectification)
- Have your data deleted (erasure)
- Have the use of your data restricted
- Receive the data you have given us in a machine-readable format and have it moved to another service (data portability)
- Object to processing based on our legitimate interests
- Withdraw your consent at any time, where processing is based on consent, without affecting the processing done before you withdrew it

To use these rights, email us at gnollhack@hyvanmielenpelit.fi. We answer within one month.

You can also act yourself on the **Personal Data** page of your account settings:

- **Download** gives you a file with your account details, your Gnoll Overseer chats, and your Terms of Service acceptance records. The text of a confidential Overseer chat is not included in the file.
- **Delete** deletes your account. Some deletion requests are reviewed by an administrator before they are completed. You are told when your request is under review, and you receive an email when the deletion is completed or declined.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Finland, it is the [Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman](https://tietosuoja.fi/en).

## 8. Security

The server stores your password only as a hash. You can turn on two-factor sign-in in your account settings. Error reports leave out credentials and the content of the forms you submit, as section 2.6 describes.

## 9. Children

If you are a parent or guardian and want a child's data deleted, email us at gnollhack@hyvanmielenpelit.fi.
