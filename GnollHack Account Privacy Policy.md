When you register and use a GnollHack account, we are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection and Registration

When you register an account with us, we store the following information as your unique identifiers:
* **Username**
* **Email address**

This information is used solely for authentication and account management purposes.

## Analytics and Cookies

account.gnollhack.com uses Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to count visits and see how the website is used. These tags load only if you accept optional cookies in the cookie notice.

If you accept, Google receives:

- the addresses of the pages you view and of the page before, without account identifiers, codes or email addresses;
- a random identifier kept in the Google Analytics cookies;
- information about your device and browser;
- the IP address that every request carries, from which Google derives an approximate location.

According to Google, Google Analytics does not store individual IP addresses: it uses the address to derive the approximate location and discards it after use.

You can change your choice at any time under **Cookie Settings** in the footer of the website. Details are in the [[GnollHack Account Cookie Policy]].
