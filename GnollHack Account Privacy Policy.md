When you register and use a GnollHack account, we are committed to protecting your privacy.

## Data Collection and Registration

When you register an account with us, we store the following information as your unique identifiers:

- **Username**
- **Email address**

This information is used for authentication and account management. We also store:

- The time your account was created and the time you last signed in, from the website or from the game
- The version of the terms of service you accepted
- Your Junethack user name, if you add one to your account
- For each request from the GnollHack app, your IP address, your user name, the address of the request, the data sent, and the result; and the IP address of failed sign-ins

## Analytics and Cookies

account.gnollhack.com uses Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to count visits and see how the website is used. These tags load only if you accept optional cookies in the cookie notice.

If you accept, Google receives:

- the addresses of the pages you view and of the page before, without account identifiers, codes or email addresses;
- a random identifier kept in the Google Analytics cookies;
- information about your device and browser;
- the IP address that every request carries, from which Google derives an approximate location.

According to Google, Google Analytics does not store individual IP addresses: it uses the address to derive the approximate location and discards it after use.

You can change your choice at any time under **Cookie Settings** in the footer of the website. Details are in the [[GnollHack Account Cookie Policy]].

## Data Retention and Account Deletion

We keep your account data until you delete your account. You can delete your account on the **Personal Data** page of your account settings. Some deletion requests are reviewed by an administrator before they are completed.

When your account is deleted, your user name, email address, and account details are deleted, and your game data is deleted or anonymized as described in the **[[GnollHack App Privacy Policy]]**. The server's request logs are part of its security audit trail and are kept, but your account and user name are removed from them.

## App-Related Data

For detailed information about what data is collected from the GnollHack applications while logged into your account, please refer to the **[[GnollHack App Privacy Policy]]**.
