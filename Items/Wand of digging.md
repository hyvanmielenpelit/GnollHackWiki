## Wand
- **Weight:** 0.4 lbs
- **Base value:** 150 gold
- **Skill:** Wand
- **Wand effect range:** 50'
- **Charges:** Yes
- **Maximum charges:** 20
- **Recharging type:** Rechargeable normally as wand

## Description

Shoots a digging ray that causes stone on its way to vanish.

Zapped downward, it digs a hole through the floor that you fall through to the level below, which makes it a quick emergency escape. On the bottom level of a dungeon branch, such as [[/Dungeon/Mines' End]], and on special levels whose floor is too hard to dig, it digs only a pit. Next to water or lava, the hole may fill with water or lava instead. It makes no hole while you stand on stairs, a ladder, an altar, a throne, a fountain or a sink, and you do not fall through while levitating, flying or held by a monster.
