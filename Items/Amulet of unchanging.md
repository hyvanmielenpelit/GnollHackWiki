## Magical Amulet

- **Weight:** 0.3 lbs
- **Base value:** 150 gold
- **Base material:** Iron

## Conferred powers

1. Unchange when worn

## Item properties

1. Polymorph resistant

## Description

While you wear it, a polymorph trap, or a potion, wand or spell of polymorph, cannot change your form, and if you are already polymorphed, the polymorph does not wear off. If your hit points in the polymorphed form go to 0, you do not return to your normal form: you die instead.
