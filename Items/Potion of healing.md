## Magical Potion

- **Weight:** 0.8 lbs
- **Base value:** 100 gold
- **Base material:** Glass
- **Healing amount:** 12d6 hit points (18d6 if blessed, 6d6 if cursed)
- **Maximum health gained:** +1 if blessed at max
- **Cures sickness:** If blessed
- **Cures blindness:** If not cursed
- **Cures hallucination:** No
- **Cures stun:** No
- **Cures confusion:** No

## Description

Restores a small amount of hit points.
