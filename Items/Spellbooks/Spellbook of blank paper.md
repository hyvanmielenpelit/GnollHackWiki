## Spellbook

- **Weight:** 3.1 lbs
- **Base value:** 32 gold
- **Base material:** Paper

## Description

A [[/Items/magic marker]] writes a spellbook on it. A spellbook becomes a spellbook of blank paper when you dip it into a fountain other than a fountain of poison, into a sink, or into a pool, unless it is greased or indestructible, like the Book of the Dead; see [[/Fountains]].
