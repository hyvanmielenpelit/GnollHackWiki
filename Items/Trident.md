## Melee Weapon: Trident

- **Weight:** 1.6 lbs
- **Base value:** 5 gold
- **Base material:** Iron
- **Skill:** Thrusting weapon
- **Hands to use:** Single-handed
- **Reach:** Can be applied to hit at range like a spear — see [[/Melee Weapons]] note 1
- **Base damage - Small:** 1d6+1 (avg 4.5)
- **Base damage - Large:** 3d4 (avg 7.5)
- **Enchantable:** Yes
- **Safe enchantable level:** +10 or below
