## Tool
- **Weight:** 0.1 lbs
- **Base value:** 125 gold
- **Base material:** Plastic
- **Charges:** Yes
- **Maximum charges:** 100
- **Recharging type:** Rechargeable as special magical tool

## Description

Magical writing tool that can be used to write scrolls on [[scrolls of blank paper|/Items/scroll of blank paper]] and spellbooks on [[spellbooks of blank paper|/Items/Spellbooks/spellbook of blank paper]].

## Writing

- A scroll can be written only on a scroll of blank paper, and a spellbook only on a spellbook of blank paper. A spellbook cannot be written on a scroll.
- Blank paper, the scroll of supreme diabolism, the Book of the Dead and the Book of Modron cannot be written at all. The spellbooks of wish, call Bahamut, armageddon and call Demogorgon need more ink than a magic marker can hold, so they cannot be written either, and trying can empty your marker.
- Any other scroll or spellbook type that you have identified can be written by its name, or by its label or cover, if your marker has enough ink.
- A scroll type that you have not identified can be written by its label if you carry a scroll of that type, and by its label or the name you gave it if you have named the type.
- A spellbook type that you have not identified cannot be written by its cover or by the name you gave it, and nothing is used up.
- Any other attempt usually fails: at Luck 0 it succeeds 1 time in 15, or 1 time in 5 for a Wizard, and your Luck changes the chance. The ink is used up even when it fails. A failed scroll disappears, and a failed spellbook stays blank.
- Writing a spellbook while blind is impossible, and writing a scroll while blind usually fails.
