## Scroll

- **Weight:** 0.2 lbs
- **Base value:** 60 gold
- **Base material:** Paper

## Description

A [[/Items/magic marker]] writes a scroll on it; it cannot be used to write a spellbook. A scroll becomes a scroll of blank paper when you dip it into a fountain, a sink, or a pool, unless it is greased, indestructible, or a scroll of mail; see [[/Fountains]].
