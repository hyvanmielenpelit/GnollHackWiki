> 👉 **The settings on GnollHack's Settings screen are available only in the modern ports.**

## 💡 Built-In Setting Descriptions

You do not need this page to find out what a setting does. Every setting on the Settings screen carries its own description inside the game:

- **Tap or click a setting's name** (not its switch) to open a popup with a full description of what the setting does and what each value means.
- **On desktop**, hovering over a setting's name shows a short one-line summary as a tooltip, and the mouse cursor changes to an info cursor over any name that has one.

The Settings screen itself is a single scrolling page. The headings below are the sections you scroll through, in the order they appear in the game.

## 🐺 Gnoll Overseer Settings

These settings control the core in-game behavior of the [[Gnoll Overseer|/Guides/Introduction to Gnoll Overseer]], the optional in-game AI advisor. The Overseer is free and fully opt-in: nothing is sent to it during play until you open it. Opening it from a game sends a snapshot of your game (if **Send Game Context** is on), and the Overseer greets you right away.

| Setting Name | Options / Values | Default | Description |
| :----------- | :--------------: | :-----: | :---------- |
| **Send Game Context** | On / Off | On | Determines whether a snapshot of your current game (character attributes, inventory, surrounding dungeon tiles, and recent messages) is sent to the Overseer when you open it during gameplay. With this off, the Overseer can answer only general questions. |
| **Client Data Access** | On / Off | On | Determines whether the Overseer may request additional data from your game client, such as the full message history. This data is sent to your AI provider for processing. |
| **Data Consent** | Accepted / None | None | Shows whether you have accepted the AI data processing disclosure. This is not a toggle: press **Revoke** to withdraw your consent, and the disclosure will appear again the next time you open the Overseer. |

> 💡 **Whether the Overseer may give spoilers, how detailed its answers are, choosing an AI model, providing your own API key (BYOK), and managing conversation history are all set in the Overseer web interface at [overseer.gnollhack.com](https://overseer.gnollhack.com), not in these settings.** See [[/Guides/Advanced Guide to Gnoll Overseer]] and [[Overseer AI Providers]].
