> 👉 **Sacrificing a corpse of your own race turns a Lawful or Neutral altar Chaotic and summons a demon lord on a Chaotic or Moloch altar, and unless you are Chaotic, your own god punishes you for it.**

Your race decides what counts: a human offering a human corpse, an elf offering an elf corpse, and so on. What happens depends on your alignment and on the altar.

## 🛕 High Altars

On a high altar, the altar's god strikes you directly unless you and the altar are both Chaotic, or the altar is Moloch's. This is the same punishment as trying to convert a high altar, and it often kills: a lightning bolt hits you unless you have reflection or shock immunity, then a disintegration beam unless you resist disintegration, and the god sends three minions after you as well.

## ⚡ The Non-Chaotic Penalty

On any other altar, a Lawful or Neutral character is warned *"You'll regret this infamous offense!"*, loses 1 Wisdom, 5 points of alignment record and 5 Luck, and adds 3 to their god's anger. That god then smites them, except inside Gehennom; [[Sacrifice Offering]] lists what the smiting can do.

A Chaotic character gains 5 points of alignment record instead.

## ⚖️ Lawful and Neutral Altars

The altar becomes Chaotic (*"The altar is stained with human blood."* for a human), and any priest attending it becomes hostile. No demon lord appears.

## 😈 Chaotic and Moloch Altars

On a Chaotic altar or Moloch's altar, the sacrifice summons a demon lord. In Gehennom it is Demogorgon, or a random chaotic demon lord if Demogorgon cannot appear, for example because he already exists. Outside Gehennom it is always a random chaotic demon lord. Unless you have fear resistance, the arrival paralyzes you for 3 turns (*"You are terrified, and unable to move."*).

| Your Alignment | Altar | What Happens to the Altar | Demon Lord | Luck |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **Lawful or Neutral** | Chaotic | *"The blood floods the altar, which vanishes in a black cloud!"* Any attending priest becomes hostile. | Always hostile | -5 |
| **Lawful or Neutral** | Moloch's | *"The blood covers the altar!"* The altar stays. | Always hostile | -7 |
| **Chaotic** | Chaotic | *"The blood covers the altar!"* The altar stays. | See below | +2 |
| **Chaotic** | Moloch's | *"The blood covers the altar!"* The altar stays. | See below | -2 |

The Lawful and Neutral Luck figures include the 5 Luck of the penalty above.

### Chaotic Character Demon Hostility

A Chaotic character's first demon lord summoned this way is always peaceful (*"Luckily for you, [demon lord] appears to be pleased with your sacrifice."*). Each later one is more likely to be hostile (*"You feel you have overstayed your welcome with the Lords of the Abyss."*), and the higher your alignment record, the lower the chance:

| Alignment Record Rank | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th |
| --------------------- | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Pious (20)            | ~13.3% | ~18.8% | ~26.7% | ~41.7% |
| Devout (14)           | ~16.7% | 25.0%  | 40.0%  | ~83.3% |
| Fervent (9)           | ~22.2% | 37.5%  | 80.0%  | ~83.3% |
| Strident (4)          | ~33.3% | 75.0%  | 80.0%  | ~83.3% |
| Normal (0)            | ~66.7% | 75.0%  | 80.0%  | ~83.3% |
| Negative (< 0)        | 80.0%  | ~85.7% | ~88.9% | ~90.9% |

## 👹 In Demon Form

If you are polymorphed into a demon, the game says *"You find the idea very satisfying."* and nothing else changes: every effect on this page still applies.
