> 👉 **GnollBench is the Gnoll Overseer's own AI benchmark.**

## 📊 What GnollBench Is For

GnollBench exists first of all to find and fix weaknesses in the Overseer itself, not merely to rank one AI model above another. Every run reports three results — Intelligence, Speed, and Cost — so a model's strengths and its price are visible side by side.
