## Description

Chaotic is generally thought to be the evil alignment. You do not suffer penalty for committing "evil" actions in the game.

## Alignment Traits

- Can sacrifice own race
- Chaotic creatures may be peaceful to you, unless your race is always hostile with theirs: gnolls and orcs, for example, are generated hostile to a human. Creatures that are always peaceful, such as shopkeepers, priests and the watch, and quest leaders and quest guardians are generated peaceful whatever your race. Your race's page lists the races yours is always hostile with.
