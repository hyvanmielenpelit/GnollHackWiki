> 👉 **The Gnoll Overseer supports multiple AI models from Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI. As of Overseer v1.1.1, there are three system models available. This page highlights how to choose the right model to use with the Gnoll Overseer.**

## Available Models

The Gnoll Overseer has now three system models available:

| Model | Thinking Level | Provider | Intelligence | Speed | Cost |
| :---- | :------------- | :------- | :----------: | :---: | :--: |
| **GPT-5.6 Luna** | **High** | Open AI | Medium | Medium | Very low |
| **Gemini 3.7 Flash** | **Medium** | Google | Medium | Fast | Medium |
| **GPT-5.6 Luna** | **Max** | Open AI | High | Slow | Low |

## Choosing the Right Model

### Daily Tasks

For normal tasks, **GPT-5.6 Luna (High)** is recommended. It is a very cheap but capable model, and also relatively fast. It should be able to answer most common questions with high accuracy.

### Urgent Tasks

If you are in a hurry and the question isn't too difficult, the recommended model to use is **Gemini 3.7 Flash (Medium)**. It is much faster that GPT-5.6 Luna models and has an intelligence comparable to *GPT-5.6 Luna* with a *High* or *Xhigh* thinking level. However, it costs more to use than GPT-5.6 Luna models, so it should be only used, when the answering speed is critical.

### Difficult Tasks

For difficult tasks, we recommend using **GPT-5.6 Luna (Max)**. It is much slower than the other two models, but also more intelligent. It is also quite cheap to use. If you have a difficult question and you have time to wait for an answer, this is the model to choose.

## Bringing Your Own Key

If you are not satisfied with the system models, you can always bring your own API key and use a provider and a model of your choice. However, in that case, you need to pay the expenses yourself.
