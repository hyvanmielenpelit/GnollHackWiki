> 👉 **Looking for a graphical way to play NetHack? This page lists graphical NetHack ports and variants that have working, ready-to-play builds and have been updated within the last two years, grouped by where you can get them.**

## 📖 About This List

Every entry runs on the NetHack code base, shows the game with graphics such as tiles, isometric views, or 3D, has a build you can install or play without compiling anything, and has been updated since November 2024. Text-only builds, source-only projects, ports that have been superseded by a newer one, and games that are merely inspired by NetHack are left out; projects that were checked but dropped are listed in [[Unmaintained and Not Listed|#unmaintained-and-not-listed]].

Entries are in alphabetical order within each category, and a project can appear in more than one category when it is distributed in several ways. Store listings, prices, and download links change over time.

**Last web scan:** November 7, 2026. Each scan rechecks every entry on this page and searches the web, including the r/nethack subreddit, for new projects.

## 🎖️ Scoreboards and Tournaments

The [NetHack Scoreboard](https://nethackscoreboard.org/) is the NetHack community's leaderboard across servers and variants, and [[Junethack]] is the community's cross-variant tournament, held every June. Both read the game logs of a fixed list of servers, such as the NAO and Hardfought public servers, so a game you play on your own device counts only if the port reports it to one of those servers. These projects on this page have their games counted:

| Port or Variant | NetHack Scoreboard | Junethack | How Games Are Counted |
| :-- | :-: | :-: | :-- |
| **GnollHack** ⭐ | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Every build except the [[Legacy Windows Version]] can post finished games to the [[GnollHack Account]] server, which both sites track. The NetHack Scoreboard needs [[Classic Mode]] and [[/Difficulties/Expert]] difficulty or higher. For Junethack, link your Junethack username to your GnollHack Account and turn on [[Tournament Mode]]. GnollHack games on the Hardfought servers are counted as well. |
| **Hardfought web terminal** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | Games on all three Hardfought servers, including the variants with tiles, such as xNetHack, EvilHack, SpliceHack, and UnNetHack. |
| **NAO web terminal** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | The NetHack Scoreboard counts both NetHack 5.0 and 3.6 games; Junethack 2026 counted only NetHack 5.0. |
| **NetHack Tiles Client** | ✅ Yes | ✅ Yes | The games run on the NAO and Hardfought servers, so they count the same as games in their web terminals. |

The other projects on this page, including NetHack 3D, VoxHack, DartHack, and the official NetHack 5.0 download, keep their games on your own device, and neither site counts them. WebHack and the online mode of nethack.app keep their games on their own servers, with high score lists that the NetHack Scoreboard does not read. EvilHack and xNetHack games count only when played on Hardfought, not in their Windows downloads.

## 🏆 Most Developed Interfaces

Judged only by the work put into the graphical interface itself, not by gameplay changes, server hosting, or other development, these projects stand out:

- **GnollHack** — Over 23,000 hand-drawn animated tiles, 37 music tracks, over 1,100 sound effects, and over 5,000 voiced lines, in a custom interface built for touch, mouse, keyboard, and game controllers. The interface has been developed since 2019 and runs on Android, iOS, Linux, macOS, Windows, and the Steam Deck.
- **NetHack 3D** — A full 3D renderer with overhead, first-person, and virtual reality views, several built-in tile sets, and sound effects, developed since 2025. It runs in web browsers and on Android, Linux, macOS, Windows, and Meta Quest, and has frequent releases.

| **To play on a phone or tablet** | GnollHack, DartHack, NetHack Android, iNethack2, or NetHack 3D |

| **To play online on a public server** | The NAO or Hardfought web terminals, or the NetHack Tiles Client |
| **To compete on the NetHack Scoreboard or in Junethack** | GnollHack, the NAO or Hardfought web terminals, or the NetHack Tiles Client |

| **To play in Japanese** | DartHack or NetHackJP |
