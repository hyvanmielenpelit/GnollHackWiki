## 💥 Missile Breakage

A dart, shuriken or boomerang that hits a monster may be destroyed by the hit. A blessed one survives against most monsters. Against a monster whose passive effect or explosion can damage the missile, other than a disenchanter, such as a balor's fire against a missile that does not resist fire, or a gas spore's explosion, a missile that hits breaks at least two times in three, and positive enchantment does not lower that; a blessed one is spared only half of those breaks. Against a disenchanter, blessing does not protect it, and the rule below adds its enchantment to its erosion instead of subtracting it, whether the enchantment is positive or negative. For one that is not blessed, against a monster without such an effect, take its erosion minus its enchantment: at 0 it breaks 5% of the time, at 1 about 6%, at 2 it is 50%, at 3 about 67%, rising from there, and below 0 a little less than 5%. A javelin, throwing axe or aklys has no such chance.
