On a [[bones level|Bones Files]], the quest artifact of the role you are playing is always an ordinary item, whatever role the dead character had, while another role's keeps its powers unless it already exists in your game.

You cannot [[wish|Wishing Guide]] for the quest artifact of the role you are playing. The wish is used up: the artifact appears in your hands for a moment and disappears, or, once it already exists in your game, you get only an ordinary item of its kind. Another role's quest artifact can be wished for like any other artifact, with the same chance of failure when several artifacts already exist; if it already exists in your game, you get only an ordinary item of its kind.
