## Tool: Grail - Artifact

- **Weight:**                 0.9 lbs
- **Base value:**             3000 gold
- **Material:**               Wooden
- **Effect:**     1000
- **Maximum charges:**        2
- **Recharging type:**        Rechargeable up to five times

## Artifact properties

1. Alignment is lawful
2. Recharge itself when invoked
    * Repowers over 1500 rounds
3. Magical protection when carried

## Description

Heals a target for 1000 hit points and 500 mana

## Notes

The Holy Grail is the Archaeologist crowning gift.

The Grail is empty when you receive it, so it starts at 0 charges. Invoke it to fill it to its maximum of 2 charges; after that you must wait 1500 rounds before you can invoke it again. Invoking does not heal anyone. To heal, apply the Grail and choose a direction (yourself, or a living companion next to you), or quaff from it. Its contents burn undead and demons instead of healing them, including your own companions and yourself if you are polymorphed into one, and non-living creatures such as golems are unaffected. Each use spends one charge, even when it heals nothing, and an empty Grail does nothing.
