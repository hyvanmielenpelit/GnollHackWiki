> 👉 **The *Gnoll Overseer* is an optional in-game AI assistant that utilizes third-party Large Language Models (LLMs) to provide helpful hints and answer questions about game mechanics via specialized custom tools. It opens on its own chat page separate from the rest of the game.**

## 🤖 AI Providers

The Gnoll Overseer backend service connects to the following external third-party AI providers:

- **OpenAI**: [https://openai.com](https://openai.com)
- **Anthropic**: [https://www.anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com)
- **Google AI**: [https://ai.google](https://ai.google)
