> 👉 **The Gnoll Overseer is a free, optional AI advisor that uses frontier AI models to answer your GnollHack questions, both in the game and at [overseer.gnollhack.com](https://overseer.gnollhack.com).**

## ✨ What It Does

A wiki page explains one mechanic at a time, which is slow going in a game as famously tough as GnollHack. The Overseer puts the pieces together for the situation you are in.

When you open it during play, it sees your character's stats, inventory, the surrounding map, and recent messages, and gives tactical advice for what is in front of you. To answer, it looks up GnollHack's own item, monster, and artifact definitions, searches the game's source code, and reads both the GnollHack and NetHack wikis, all within seconds. A question that touches monsters, resistances, potions, and dungeon hazards gets one answer instead of ten articles to read.

Spoiler protection is on by default, so the Overseer does not tell you about things you have not discovered yet. It can also read one of your past dumplogs and explain what went wrong.

## ℹ️ Requirements

The Overseer needs a [[GnollHack Account]] and an internet connection. It is free with the system models; if you use your own API key, your AI provider bills you. You can open it from the in-game menu, the About page, or the web. It uses models from Anthropic, Google, and OpenAI.
